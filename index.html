How a custom order works

No surprises. You see and approve the design before a single drop of resin is poured.

  1. Share your idea

    Tell us the occasion, names, colours and budget on WhatsApp or the enquiry form. Photos and Pinterest links help.

  2. Approve the design

    We send a mock-up with the final price and timeline. We start only after you say yes.

  3. Handcrafting

    Each piece is poured, layered and finished by hand in our studio, with progress photos on request.

  4. Curing and checks

    Resin cures fully for 48 to 72 hours, then we inspect for clarity, edges, finish and spelling.

  5. Gift-ready delivery

    Wrapped, padded and shipped with tracking. Your gift arrives ready to hand over.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I customise?

Almost everything: names, dates, colours, fonts, shapes, sizes and embedded items such as photos or flowers. Share your idea and we will tell you what works best in resin or decor, and send a mock-up.

How long does an order take?

Small gifts take 5 to 8 days, larger resin pieces 8 to 12 days, and wedding props 2 to 4 weeks. Resin needs 48 to 72 hours to cure, so we do not rush it. Tell us your event date and we will work backwards from it.

Can you preserve my wedding flowers?

Yes. Send fresh flowers or the varmala within 24 hours of the ceremony, ideally in a cool place. We dry and set them in resin as a frame, tray or keepsake. Full instructions come with your confirmation.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, to every serviceable pincode. Pieces are double-padded and sent by tracked courier. Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 days after dispatch, and we share the tracking link on WhatsApp.

How does payment work?

A 50% advance confirms the order and begins production once you approve the design. The balance is due before dispatch. We accept UPI, bank transfer and major cards. Corporate orders can be invoiced with GST.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send us a photo and the unboxing video within 48 hours, and we will repair or remake the piece at no cost to you.
